PROJECT BRIEF — CO-PACKING ENGAGEMENT

Prepared: October 15, 2026
Client: Bituin Foods Corporation
Vendor: Sari-Saya Packaging Solutions Inc.

SCOPE
Co-packing of 120,000 snack units for the holiday season, delivered in
three batches between 11/05/2026 and 12/20/2026.

BUDGET
PHP 350,000 covering materials and labor; overrun capped at 8%.
A deposit of $6,000 is due upon signing of the co-packing agreement.

CONFIDENTIALITY
Recipes, supplier pricing, and the distribution plan for 2027 are strictly
confidential and may not be shared outside the project team of both
companies.
